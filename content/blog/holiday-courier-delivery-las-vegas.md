November and December change how offices work. Clients close early, staff take time off, and year-end paperwork, client packages and last orders all need to move before the break. A little planning keeps those deliveries on schedule and on budget. This guide covers holiday courier delivery in Las Vegas: which runs you can book online at a flat price, which ones need a quote, and how to make sure someone is there to receive them.

## Regular days, holidays and after hours

The first question is when the delivery happens, because that decides how it's priced.

- **Weekdays during business hours:** flat table prices you can see and pay online. Choose **Rush**, picked up and delivered within **60 to 90 minutes**, or **Same Day**: order by **2 PM** and it's delivered by the end of the business day.
- **Holidays, weekends and after hours:** yes, we deliver then too, including emergencies. These runs are **priced by quote**. Tell us what you need and we confirm the price.

For a quote, we reply by email with a price and a secure payment link. Quotes are valid for **24 hours**, so ask for one close to the day you need it.

## Plan what you can with Same Day

Many holiday deliveries aren't emergencies. They just have to arrive before an office closes. Same Day is the right fit for those, and it costs **$10 less** than Rush for every item size and distance. For a trip of up to 7.5 miles:

- **Envelope (any size):** $39 Same Day, $49 Rush
- **Medium box (up to 25 lb):** $49 Same Day, $59 Rush
- **Big box (25 to 50 lb):** $59 Same Day, $69 Rush

Each longer distance band, 7.5 to 15 miles and then 15 to 25 miles, adds $10. The full table is on the [services and pricing page](/services).

The key is the **2 PM cutoff**. When you know a document or package has to go out before a holiday, book it in the morning and choose the time it will be ready for pickup. If you're unsure which speed you need, read [Rush vs. Same Day: how to choose](/blog/rush-vs-same-day-delivery-las-vegas).

## Keep Rush for the real last-minute runs

Rush is for the moments when someone is waiting on the other end today. Around the holidays that often looks like:

- Signed pages a client needs before their office closes for the break.
- A part or supply that has to arrive before your team leaves for the day.
- A document that was forgotten in the rush to finish the year.

There are no waiting-time fees. Have the item sealed and labeled before you book so the handoff takes seconds.

## Make sure someone can receive it

Around the holidays, the office you're sending to may be closed or short-staffed. If the recipient is unavailable or refuses the item, we contact you, and a return trip or second delivery attempt is quoted separately. A few minutes of checking avoids that:

1. **Confirm the recipient's hours.** Ask whether the office is closing early or shutting for the week.
2. **Name a person, not just a business.** Add a contact name and phone for the drop-off, and a backup if the main contact is out.
3. **Add receiving instructions.** Put the suite, floor, gate code or receiving door in the instructions box when you [book online](/order).
4. **Send earlier when you can.** Our delivery windows apply under normal conditions, so give yourself room when the day really matters.

## Client packages and year-end gifts

Many businesses send packages to clients and partners at the end of the year. A few things to know before you pack:

- **Size it by weight.** A medium box is up to 25 lb packed, a big box is 25 to 50 lb. Anything heavier or oversized is priced by quote. Our [packing guide for envelopes and boxes](/blog/how-to-pack-for-same-day-courier-las-vegas) covers how to pack and label them.
- **Check what we can't carry.** We don't transport alcohol, perishable food or beverages, or money, among other items. The full list is in our [Terms of Use](/terms).
- **More than one recipient means a quote.** Several items, several stops or a round trip in one run are priced by quote, so you can send one request for the whole route.
- **Think about value.** Every delivery is covered up to **$100**. For anything worth more, a higher declared value can be arranged in advance by quote.
