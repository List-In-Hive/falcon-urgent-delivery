When a signature is the last thing standing between a deal and a deadline, the document has to move fast and you need to know it arrived. Law offices, title companies and escrow teams across the Las Vegas valley use a local courier for exactly that. Here's a short checklist to make each run smooth, and what to expect from Falcon.

## Before you book: a five-point checklist

1. **Seal it and label it.** Put the recipient's name, company and suite number on the envelope itself, not only on the order.
2. **Name the person who can receive it.** Front desks change hands. Add the name of the attorney, assistant or officer to the delivery instructions, plus any gate code or suite entrance.
3. **Pick the right speed.** Rush is picked up and delivered within 60 to 90 minutes. Same Day costs less if you order by 2 PM and it can arrive by the end of the business day.
4. **Check the distance.** Prices are based on driving miles from pickup to drop-off: up to 7.5 miles, 7.5 to 15, or 15 to 25.
5. **Think about value.** Every delivery is covered up to $100. If the contents are worth more, ask for a quote so a higher declared value can be arranged in advance.

## What proof you get

Knowing a document arrived matters as much as getting it there. With every Falcon delivery you receive two emails:

- one when the envelope is **picked up**, and
- one when it's **delivered**, with a **photo**, the **delivery time** and the **name of the person who received it**.

Keep that email with the file. It answers the "did they get it?" question in seconds.

## Rush or Same Day for legal work?

Use **Rush** for anything with a clock on it today: signature pages for a closing, documents a client is waiting to sign in person, or originals someone needs in hand within the hour.

Use **Same Day** for paperwork that needs to arrive before close of business: returning signed originals, sending copies to another office, or routine exchanges you can plan in the morning. Read more in [Rush vs. Same Day: how to choose](/blog/rush-vs-same-day-delivery-las-vegas).
