A rush delivery is only as fast as the handoff. When the item is packed, sealed and labeled before the driver arrives, pickup takes seconds and the item reaches the other side of the valley in the same shape it left. This guide covers how to pack envelopes and boxes for a same-day courier in Las Vegas, and what to have ready when you book.

## Start with the right size

Falcon prices every delivery by what you send, how fast it needs to go and how far:

- **Envelope:** documents and anything flat, any size.
- **Medium box:** up to **25 lb** packed.
- **Big box:** from **25 to 50 lb** packed.

![An envelope, a medium box and a big box side by side, with their weight limits and starting prices](/images/blog-package-sizes.webp)

Weigh the item after it's packed, because padding and the box add weight. Anything over 50 lb, oversized, or split across several boxes is priced by quote instead.

## Packing documents

- **Use one sturdy envelope per delivery.** Everything for the same recipient goes in the same envelope. Two separate envelopes count as more than one item.
- **Keep originals flat.** Don't fold signed originals or certificates. For anything that mustn't bend, slide it into a folder or a rigid mailer first.
- **Seal it.** Close the envelope fully so nothing slips out on the way.
- **Write the recipient on the front.** The business name and the person it's for are enough.

## Packing boxes

1. **Pick a box that fits.** A box that's too big lets items shift; one that's too small strains the seams. Leave a little room for padding.
2. **Fill the gaps.** Use packing paper, bubble wrap or air pillows so nothing moves when you shake the box gently.
3. **Heavy items at the bottom.** Put lighter, more delicate items on top.
4. **Wrap fragile pieces one by one.** Glass, ceramics and small electronics each get their own layer of wrap.
5. **Tape every seam.** Run tape along the middle and both edges of the top and bottom flaps.

Las Vegas heat is hard on packages. Falcon carries everything in an air-conditioned van, but sealing the box well keeps dust out and contents secure from door to door.

## Label it for a fast handoff

A clear label saves time at both ends. Put it on the top of the box, and remove or cover any old shipping labels so there's only one address on it.

![A shipping label showing what to include: recipient business, contact name and phone, suite or floor, and your reference number](/images/blog-packing-label.webp)

Then give the driver the same details when you book. On the [booking page](/order), the drop-off section asks for the recipient business, contact name and phone. Add the suite, gate code or receiving door in the instructions box.

## Check the value before you book

Every delivery is covered up to **$100**. If what you're sending is worth more, [request a quote](/contact?topic=quote) so a higher declared value can be arranged before pickup.

## What we can't carry

Some items can't go in the van, however well they're packed:

- Hazardous materials and explosives
- Firearms, weapons and weapon parts
- Alcohol, recreational drugs and anything illegal
- Money and stolen goods
- Animals
- Perishable food or beverages

The full list is in our [Terms of Use](/terms).
